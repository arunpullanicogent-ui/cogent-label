COGENT AUTOMATION LABEL PRINTER

1. Extract this ZIP to a normal folder on your Windows PC.
2. Double-click start.bat.
3. Open http://localhost:3000 if the browser does not open automatically.
4. Keep the black PowerShell window open while using the app.
5. Upload your PDFs and logo. Label size is 50 x 30 mm.

For full instructions, read README.md.
